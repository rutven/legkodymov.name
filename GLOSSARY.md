# legkodymov.name

Sergei Legkodymov's developer site: a catalog of his apps with the public links app stores and users need.

## Language

**App**:
One product the Developer offers to users, listed on this site; may be available on several Platforms.
_Avoid_: service, project, product

**Platform**:
A way to use an App — web, Android or Telegram.
_Avoid_: client, version, edition

**Developer**:
Sergei Legkodymov, the person who publishes the apps; named exactly so everywhere.
_Avoid_: Sergey, author, company

**Operator**:
The party an App's privacy policy names as responsible for its users' data — the Developer personally, or his sole proprietorship "Sergei Legkodymov PR Beograd".
_Avoid_: owner, company, controller

**Developer email**:
`apps@legkodymov.name` — the address stores and partners use to reach the Developer.
_Avoid_: contact email, personal email

**App page**:
The page on this site dedicated to one app, holding its description and public links.
_Avoid_: product page, landing page

**Support address**:
The email users of a specific app write to for help; lives on the app's own domain when it has one.
_Avoid_: contact, helpdesk

**Linked policy**:
A privacy policy published on an app's own site; this site only links to it.
_Avoid_: external policy

**Hosted policy**:
A privacy policy published on this site because the app has no site of its own.
_Avoid_: local policy, fallback policy

**Deletion page**:
A public page explaining how a user deletes their account and data for one app, and what happens to it.
_Avoid_: delete-account info, data deletion URL

**Deletion request**:
An email from a user asking to delete their account; honored only when sent from the account's own address, within 30 days.
_Avoid_: removal request, GDPR request
